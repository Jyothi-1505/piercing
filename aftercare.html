AFTERCARE GUIDE

Care for your new beginning.

Thoughtful aftercare helps your piercing heal beautifully. Follow our simple guide to keep your piercing clean, comfortable and cared for.

Start the Guide
AURIA / CARE GUIDE 01
THE FIRST 24 HOURS

Start with gentle care.

The first day sets the tone for healing. Keep your new piercing clean, undisturbed and comfortable.

01

Clean gently

Clean the piercing as recommended by your piercer using a suitable sterile saline solution.

02

Keep hands away

Avoid touching, twisting or rotating your jewelry unless your aftercare instructions specifically require it.

03

Let it rest

Protect the area from unnecessary pressure, friction and snagging while your piercing begins to settle.

CLEAN / PROTECT / REST AURIA AFTERCARE
AURIA / DAILY CARE 03
DAILY CARE ROUTINE

Keep it simple.

A gentle routine is all your piercing needs. Consistency matters more than complicated steps.

01

Clean

Use a suitable sterile saline solution according to your piercer's guidance.

02

Dry

Allow the area to dry naturally or gently pat around it with a clean disposable paper product.

03

Protect

Minimize friction, pressure and snagging from clothing, hair or accessories.

04

Be patient

Healing takes time. Stay consistent and follow the individual advice from your AURIA piercer.

CONSISTENCY OVER COMPLEXITY AURIA / 2026
WHAT TO AVOID

Give your piercing room to heal.

Healing is easier when you keep unnecessary pressure, friction and interference away.

01

Don't twist

Avoid rotating or playing with your jewelry while it heals.

02

Avoid pressure

Reduce contact from sleeping, headphones and accessories.

03

Skip harsh products

Avoid alcohol, peroxide and other irritating products.

04

Don't rush

Give your piercing enough time to heal before changing jewelry.

04
LESS IS MORE.
Simple care creates better healing.
